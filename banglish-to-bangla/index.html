Yes. You get 3 free conversions a day here and in the NativeToEnglish apps, up to 1,500 characters each. No sign-up. Pro gives 500 a day.

How does it convert Banglish?

It reads what you typed in English letters, works out the Bangla words you meant, and writes them in proper Bangla script, fixing spelling on the way.

Can I use it inside other apps?

Yes. The NativeToEnglish Android app adds an entry to the long-press menu in any app, and the Chrome extension adds a button to every text box. Type in Banglish, tap once, and it is replaced where you wrote it.

No. Only the text you convert is sent, only when you press the button, and it is not stored or used to train AI.
